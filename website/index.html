It only answers from what you are allowed to read

Every document carries a classification, an owner and an access level. Retrieval checks your role before a single paragraph reaches the model.

Public

Press releases, published product sheets.

Internal

Policies, SOPs, internal product knowledge.

Confidential

Pricing, customer proposals, security standards.

Restricted

Board material, M&A, personal data.

Governance built into the platform, not left in a policy document.

  • Single sign-on and role-based access through your enterprise identity provider, with MFA.
  • Audit trail for every prompt, model, agent, tool and knowledge source used.
  • PII protection and DLP on what goes in and what comes out.
  • Human-in-the-loop for any action with external impact.
  • Model and prompt policy set by the AI Governance Committee.
  • Cost and usage tracking per user, conversation, model and agent.

What the MVP has to prove

Success is counted in work finished with SCALEGPT’s help, such as proposals, business cases and analyses completed, not in the number of chats.

Adoption

  • 500 registered users
  • 300 monthly active
  • 100 daily active

Knowledge

  • 1,000 documents indexed
  • 70%+ of RAG answers with usable sources

Quality

  • 90%+ citation accuracy
  • 85%+ agent task success
  • Under 5% critical hallucination

Productivity

  • 20–30% less time on selected document tasks
  • 30% of active users on a specialist agent

These are initial engineering targets for the MVP, not measured results.
